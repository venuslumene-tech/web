About Venus Lumene

We believe the everyday is deserving of beauty, attention, and feeling.

Art & atmosphere

Objects for a life that is looked at closely.

Venus Lumene is an evolving world of paper goods, prints, gifts and home objects. We start with cards because a note is one of the most intimate things we can make: an image and a few words travelling from one pair of hands to another.

Our work moves at an unhurried pace, making space for tactility, irregularity and the stories an object gathers when it is used.

The approach

Quietly expressive. Never overdone.

We borrow from editorial design, still-life painting, rooms with good afternoon light and the strange beauty of a handwritten note. The result is art that feels like it has always belonged near you.
