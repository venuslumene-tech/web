Shipping & returns.

Every order is packed with care from our studio. The following is a clear guide to getting your pieces safely to you.

Delivery

Dispatch

Orders are prepared within 2–4 business days. Domestic orders are sent with tracked standard delivery; international availability and timing are confirmed at checkout.

Complimentary shipping

Complimentary domestic shipping applies to orders over $75. We will always email a tracking link when your parcel begins its journey.

Returns

Changed your mind?

Unopened, unused items may be returned within 14 days of delivery. As cards are a paper good, they must be returned in their original protective packaging. Please contact us before sending anything back.
